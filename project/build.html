Understanding Your Results

What Do the Numbers Mean?

Protons Identify the Element

Every element has a unique number of protons. An atom with one proton is hydrogen, while an atom with eight protons is oxygen.

Neutrons Determine the Isotope

Atoms of the same element can have different numbers of neutrons. These variations are called isotopes. Some isotopes are stable, while others are radioactive.

Electrons Affect Charge

When the number of electrons equals the number of protons, the atom is electrically neutral. Different numbers produce positively or negatively charged ions.
